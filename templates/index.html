About

Download NPAAAD or SAFDZ maps and GIS data by island group, region, province, municipality/city, or barangay. You can also upload a custom boundary.

Download Options

JPEG – Printable 4K map (3840 × 2406 px) with legend, scale bar, and area summary.

Shapefile ZIP – GIS vector format (SHP, SHX, DBF, etc.) for spatial analysis.

GeoPackage – Single-file GIS format compatible with most GIS software.

Tips

  • Leave Province blank to download the selected region.
  • Select a province, municipality, or barangay for a smaller area.
  • Use Advanced Options to clip with your own boundary.
  • Large areas may take longer to prepare.
