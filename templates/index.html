How to Download

  1. Select an island group and region.
  2. Leave Province blank for the whole region, or choose a province, municipality, or barangay.
  3. Select the NPAAAD or SAFDZ layer.
  4. Choose a format below the area fields. Your file downloads automatically when ready.
